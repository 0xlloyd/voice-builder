# AI tells

Show this list during the probe and ask the user which items make them cringe: anything they mark goes on their never-say list, and the rest stays a default to avoid unless their own material shows they genuinely do it (drawn from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing").

## Words

delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, crucial, vibrant, seamless, robust, leverage, foster, elevate, unlock, embark, showcase, underscore, intricate, realm, landscape (meaning a field), navigate (meaning handle), game-changer

## Phrases

- It's important to note that…
- In today's fast-paced world…
- Let's dive in.
- Here's the thing:
- I hope this email finds you well.
- Great question!
- plays a crucial role in…
- In conclusion,

## Habits

- "It's not X, it's Y" contrasts
- Lists of three where two or four would be truer
- A punchy one-line closer that restates the point
- Opening with a staged question or "imagine…"
- Dashes everywhere in place of commas and full stops
- Bold labels and bullet points in what should be a plain message
- Emoji dropped into otherwise serious text
- Hedging both sides when they'd simply say what they think
