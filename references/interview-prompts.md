# Interview prompts

Use these only when the user gets stuck or the material is missing a kind of situation, because a topic they chose themselves always beats a prompt.

## Story prompts

- Tell me about a time something went badly wrong, or nearly did.
- What's the best or worst day you've had at work, and what happened?
- Tell me about a time you turned out to be right when nobody believed you.

## Opinion prompts

- What's something most people get wrong about your work or your hobby?
- What small, everyday thing drives you up the wall?
- What's a hill you'd happily die on?

## Explanation prompts

- Explain what you do for a living to a friend who has no idea.
- Walk me through how to do something you're really good at.
- Talk me through a decision you made recently and why.

## Phrasing pairs

Start from these, but build most pairs from the user's own material by setting a phrase they used against the bland version an LLM would write.

- keep a record / keep the receipts
- let's circle back / let's pick this up later
- I'd recommend / I'd go with
- touch base / catch up
- thank you for your patience / thanks for bearing with me
- it's important to note / worth saying
- utilise / use
- I'm excited to share / so, news

## Everyday moments

Ask "how would you actually say this?" for any the material didn't cover, and keep the answers word for word.

- Turning down a meeting or an invite
- Pushing back on someone's idea
- Saying thanks for a favour
- Apologising for running late
- Asking someone for help
- Giving bad news
- Congratulating someone
